# Firewood office workflow security review

Reviewed October 29, 2026. **Accept the inactive local workflow package for focused release rehearsal. Keep production, provider execution and activation unchanged.** This review covers the office approval/cancellation extension, authenticated command adapter, separate Website office protocol and executable synthetic tests. It is not a native production installation, live refund test or new recovery certificate.

## Engineering decisions

| Boundary | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Office authority | Use the actual authenticated session and existing effective owner/admin predicate. Office SQL takes the existing `platform-role-management` lock before checking access. Neither a Website signing credential nor the database service role can approve prices, record a delivery window or cancel an order as an office actor. The office page must not serialize these records to estimators/payroll users merely because they can view ordinary Firewood invoices. |
| Price review | Website submits an immutable quantity/stacking/contact draft, without prices or approval assertions. Office commands reread that trusted row under lock and use the existing real approval transaction. Customer/contact substitution, fake approval IDs and changed same-key prices fail. Expiry/revocation and accepted price history remain authoritative; no new invoice is prepared until customer acceptance. |
| Cancellation | Requesting cancellation first places a durable collection and delivery hold. Confirming cancellation is a separate action over the current workflow revision and full financial snapshot. A full-eligible order with zero payment rows, zero checkout rows and no original paid evidence uses the existing guarded invoice void transaction before cancellation is finalized. Other positive balances require billing closure; they are not labeled fully cancelled. Invoice, original acceptance, capabilities, payments and audit history remain. No pending or refunded money is voided away. |
| Financial uncertainty | Creating/open/processing checkouts, pending bank payments, pending refunds, disputes and returned bank payments require review. Unknown balances, inconsistent refund totals or unmatched completed checkouts cannot become verified zeroes. A late payment can still be recorded after cancellation and creates refund review while the cancellation hold remains. |
| Existing provider work | The new live-only checkout guard blocks new collection and reopening of terminal attempts. It permits an already-started attempt to save known provider references and settle/expire, preserving recovery of the external operation. A local hold alone does not close an external Checkout. Any attempted order with a positive invoice balance stays at `billing_closure_required`, even after its checkout closes; the no-provider void exception is deliberately limited to a completely empty money/attempt history. |
| Refund eligibility | The server records request time and applies the accepted terms against a trusted recorded delivery-window start. Unscheduled and eligible 48-hour cases, Angel cancellation/cannot-fulfill and declined Angel rescheduling retain full-refund eligibility. Later customer cancellation is non-refundable. Eligibility is separate from actual returned money. Office notes/evidence are retained, not exposed in public status. |
| Date interpretation | The command adapter uses Eastern business time and rejects nonexistent/repeated daylight-saving wall times. SQL rejects non-finite timestamps. Neither a browser-supplied cancellation date nor the operator Mac's timezone decides the cutoff. |
| Protocol and downgrade | The prior canonical live contract/profile/terms and sandbox protocol stay separate. The office protocol adds exact, versioned lifecycle status with a distinct signing domain. Financial payment state remains truthful while lifecycle holds force fulfillment false. Old financial-only status cannot regain fulfillment authority over a cancellation. Actual launch must enroll only the reviewed new Website companion/worker; there is no fallback to an old worker's historical paid observation or outbox. |
| Retry and privacy | Requests retain their original identity, actor, operation and payload. Exact retry returns the original result; changed payloads and other actors cannot reuse it. Form pollution, actor fields, provider/send instructions and private public-receipt fields are rejected. Provider errors and internal diagnostics do not become customer/office payloads. |
| Activation | Both runtime factories remain unbound. No environment flag supplies admission, credentials or authority. The extension creates no source row and sends no email, initiates no payment/refund, and performs no live installation. |

## Findings corrected during review

1. **Returned bank money was initially treated as refundable historical gross.** The office summary now requires review for a previously collected bank payment that later fails or is canceled. This prevents office guidance from suggesting a second reimbursement after a bank return.
2. **Per-row refund bounds were insufficient.** Verification now bounds aggregate active/successful refund exposure per payment, checks successful Stripe refund totals, reconciles invoice balance with retained principal and verifies completed checkout ownership. Inconsistent snapshots remain unverified and cannot confirm cancellation.
3. **The first command integration had mismatched argument/result shapes.** Accepted-order RPCs require `p_revision`, and mutation receipts need explicit order/invoice binding. The adapter/SQL have been aligned and exercised through real named SQL arguments, including exact retry after a committed confirmation.
4. **Lifecycle and time needed explicit validation.** Non-active states require a positive workflow revision, cancellation cannot roll backward, and a same-version response cannot change content. Non-finite SQL dates and ambiguous/missing Eastern wall times are rejected. Price expiry must follow the actual approval time.
5. **Final cancellation originally left collectible debt.** The first candidate could mark an unpaid/refunded order cancelled while its sent invoice remained in outstanding totals. The clean full-eligible case now calls the actual retained `begin_invoice_void` and `finalize_invoice_void` inside the same office transaction. The live guard admits only the matching owned, active, finalizing void lease during a full-eligible cancellation request with zero history. Original core protections remain. A failure after void rolls the invoice status, reminder cancellation, lease, audit and acknowledgement back together. Fully refunded history or other positive balances stay requested with explicit billing-closure review; fully paid late non-refundable cancellation may finalize while retaining the paid invoice. Original amounts/balances are preserved, and the ordinary void-status reporting exclusion closes the clean debt without fake payments or report-specific hiding.

## Independent verification

The owned suite is `tests/firewood-office-security.test.mjs`: **26/26 tests passed** on Node 22.23.4 after the accounting follow-up. It executes the actual review SQL and TypeScript command adapter using PGlite, fixed role/session helpers and fictional contacts. It starts no listener, uses no credential and has no provider or sender transport. Positive SQL contacts are generated on the company-controlled domain solely in memory so the unchanged ordinary live contact guard is exercised; `.invalid` contacts remain in explicit parser fixtures.

Coverage includes private API-role denial, offboarded/non-admin denial, immutable trusted draft/price authority, forged actor/approval substitution, changed retries, finite time/policy decisions, request pollution, actual named command arguments, idempotent confirmation, original paid retention, late provider evidence, pending refund CAS, stale balances, aggregate over-refunds, collection continuation/reopen rules, public status privacy and lifecycle rollback. The accounting tests load the retained original 102 void/collection guards, independently compare the installed begin/finalize definitions with their source hashes, reject generic void without the cancellation boundary, exercise a late failure after the real void, retain fully refunded debt for genuine billing closure, and reject installation without the guarded-void prerequisites. Root-owned integration, UI and build results are reported separately in the main implementation report; this independent review does not claim their results as its own.

## Required before production admission

- Rehearse the exact current 102-based focused schema and original financial/access triggers, including simultaneous offboarding/approval, collection/cancellation, provider acknowledgment and refund-observation races. Single-session PGlite verifies behavior but does not certify concurrent native transactions.
- Read back actual SQL owners/ACLs, source identity, authenticated office routing and the precise Website companion/worker. Verify current live provider references/permissions separately; do not start another collector or bypass dedicated surcharge checks.
- Enroll all new office tables and functions in the existing schema/write/commit/truncate and paired off-device recovery topology. Obtain a fresh five-minute certificate for the actual new source. The prior live-profile capsule and certificate cannot cover the added workflow data.
- Keep refunds and customer notices deliberate. The current candidate records eligibility/holds, safely closes only clean never-collected full-eligible debt, and observes genuine shared-ledger outcomes. It is not an automatic refund or notification dispatcher. A real credit/accounting-closure workflow is still required for refunded or otherwise historical positive balances; do not bypass that hold. Full activation requires its own reviewed installation and authorization.
