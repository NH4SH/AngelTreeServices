## Result

Quote link generation/reuse, regeneration and per-link revocation now commit their token changes, activity history and private retry receipt together. Live internal-staff authorization remains required, including estimators and payroll administrators; crew, anonymous and service-only callers cannot invoke the office commands. Authenticated direct token insert/update/delete is closed. Trusted service telemetry and customer response RPCs retain their intended access.

An office action carries its original request ID, displayed quote/link version, intent and selected token. A stale quote, customer reassignment or competing link change requires review. View/open telemetry does not invalidate the review. The database takes request locks before business rows, then job → quote → token locks; multi-proposal recovery locks all selected jobs, quotes and tokens in deterministic order. Authorization is checked again after waits. Existing quote approval/work-order and customer change-request transactions are preserved, with a narrowly anchored current-customer/organization check added to both token predicates.

Receipt recovery runs before generating another encrypted candidate. An earlier generation/replacement is acknowledged without replacing again. Later revocation, replacement, expiry or customer reassignment prevents disclosure of its obsolete capability. Replaying an older revoke cannot disable a newer link. Receipts and browser recovery storage contain opaque identities and versions; they do not contain raw URLs, encrypted tokens, customer message text or email content. Current URLs come only from freshly loaded, usable, decrypted tokens whose hashes match.

The quote panel, single-proposal email replacement control and combined-proposal composer share these recovery rules. Sharing and sending are blocked during unresolved recovery or stale-state review. A confirmed action reloads current links; it does not email a customer. Existing copy/open, expiry, opened/used status, history, individual revocation and confirmation controls remain. Normal saved quote edits still appear through the existing customer link without revoking it. Closed historical proposals remain shareable without reopening their business state.

Combined-proposal **legacy link replacement** is now one transaction for up to 25 affected proposals belonging to one customer or organization. A later invalid candidate, stale selection or final receipt failure rolls back every replacement, child receipt and audit event. The original selection survives page reloads and response loss. Proposals keep separate prices, approval decisions, recipients and secure destinations; CC and the existing outbox/delivery duplicate protections are unchanged. Current email dispatch already rechecks the selected token IDs through its existing delivery guard.

One intentional cleanup change: failed normal combined-email preparation retains links already generated for retry. Its former best-effort direct revocation could disable a link that a concurrent email had just reused, and is incompatible with the reviewed authority boundary. No partial email is queued by those preparation failures. Staff can still explicitly revoke any unwanted link. This does **not** make all normal email preparation one database transaction; the atomic batch guarantee applies to the explicitly confirmed legacy-link replacement selection.
