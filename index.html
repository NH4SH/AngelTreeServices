What homeowners ask

Straight answers before you book.

Learn what affects tree-removal pricing, why arborist credentials matter, and what to expect from your estimate, property protection, and cleanup.

Why hire an ISA Certified Arborist?

An ISA Certified Arborist has demonstrated knowledge of tree biology, diagnosis, pruning, maintenance, and safe tree-care practices. That helps homeowners receive recommendations based on the tree’s health, structure, location, and potential risks—not simply whether it can be cut down. Angel Tree Services is led by an ISA Certified Arborist and backed by more than 30 years of tree-industry experience.

How much does tree removal cost?

Tree-removal cost depends on the tree’s size and condition, site access, proximity to buildings and utilities, and whether climbing, rigging, or specialized equipment is needed. Hauling, stump grinding, and cleanup also affect the scope. Because every property is different, we provide an individual written estimate that identifies the work and included services.

Can a damaged or leaning tree be saved?

Sometimes. We consider the tree’s health, structure, roots, visible defects, location, and potential risk to people and property. A lean or damaged limb does not automatically mean removal is necessary. When appropriate, responsible pruning or continued monitoring may be recommended. If removal is the safer option, we explain the concerns behind that recommendation.

Can you remove trees close to homes and landscaping?

Yes. Many of our projects involve limited work zones near homes, fences, driveways, utilities, and landscaping. Depending on the site, removals may use climbers, bucket trucks, rigging, and controlled lowering to manage branches and logs carefully. We evaluate access, nearby structures, and the available work area before planning the removal.

What does your written estimate include?

Our written estimate identifies the trees or limbs included, the type of work, how logs and brush will be handled, whether hauling and cleanup are included, and whether stump grinding is part of the price. Options and exclusions are stated so you can understand the complete scope before approving it. Request a written estimate.

Is stump grinding included with tree removal?

Stump grinding is not automatically included with removal; it must appear in the written estimate. Pricing depends on the stump’s size, location, access, surrounding obstacles, and how the grindings will be handled. Grinding reduces the visible stump below grade, but it does not remove the tree’s entire underground root system.

How do you protect my property and handle cleanup?

Protection begins with planning equipment access and how limbs and logs will move through the work zone. We consider homes, fences, driveways, lawns, and landscaping before work begins. The crew completes the debris removal and cleanup included in the written estimate. Tree work can affect turf in some conditions, so site limitations are discussed when relevant.

Are you insured, and can I request proof?

Yes. Angel Tree Services is insured for its tree-care operations, and we can provide certificates of insurance upon request. Insurance is an important consideration when work is performed around homes, vehicles, neighboring properties, and other structures. Learn more about our credentials and safety approach.
