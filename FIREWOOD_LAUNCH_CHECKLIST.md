# Firewood launch checklist

The owner approved installation, final isolated payment tests, publication and
activation after the checks pass. Approval is recorded; this checklist is not
activation authority. Live ordering, payments and sending remain off during
preparation. The public website's analytics, consent and estimate behavior stay
unchanged. No production leads or real test purchases may be submitted.

## Reviewed business terms

| Quantity | Delivered | Delivered and stacked |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Half cord | $300 | $450 |
| Full cord | $450 | $600 |
| Two cords | $840 | $990 |

Delivery is included. Stacking adds $150 once per delivery and requires
wheelbarrow access and a reasonable carry. The two-cord 30% comparison uses four
$300 half-cord orders before stacking. Online orders are limited to two cords for
personal household use in the five confirmed delivery areas. Businesses call.

Angel contacts the customer with a delivery date within a week; this is not a
promise to deliver within a week. The accepted checkout terms define the
48-hour cancellation cutoff, unscheduled and Angel-initiated full-refund cases,
and late customer cancellation. Office-approved veterans pricing must be agreed
before purchase. Payment choice and any eligible credit-card surcharge are
reviewed before authorization; debit, prepaid, bank and offline payments do not
receive that credit-card surcharge.

## Installed-system evidence required before activation

- [ ] CRM owner installs the exact reviewed shared schema, restricted roles,
  grants, guarded native proof and combined source/recovery profile. Website
  request/worker containers use the existing private service network. PostgreSQL
  has no host or public listener.
- [ ] Exact root-owned component metadata, immutable source manifest, native
  proof closure, mounted credential fingerprints and seeded order-store identity
  agree. Environment booleans cannot substitute for admission.
- [ ] Independently verified fresh paired backups include all accepted orders,
  invoices, payments, credits, confirmation/outbox identities and required files.
  Check automatic captures, restart/fence behavior and restoration without
  duplicate payment, invoice or confirmation effects.
- [ ] Separate acceptance host, portal, cookie, assets, API, database, HMAC and
  test-account bindings are observed and recorded. Planned names or a test flag
  cannot authorize a provider-ready runtime. Production keys/data stay separate.
- [ ] Fresh genuine Stripe tests cover delivered/stacked totals, supported
  payment options, eligible credit fee and fee-free alternatives, failed/pending
  settlement, webhook replay, refunds, disputes and cancellation holds.
- [ ] Controlled customer/owner confirmation tests go only to the approved owner
  inbox, retain separate stable identities, and verify delivery and uncertain-send
  retry/restart behavior. Existing receipt history is preserved.
- [ ] Live merchant identity/capabilities, narrowly scoped credentials, permanent
  HTTPS route and owner pause/recovery procedures are verified while ordering is
  still closed. No live charge is used as a readiness test.

## Publication and launch

- [ ] Recheck the latest repository head and preserve newer changes. Commit only
  reviewed source/tests/public-page files. Skip both automatic repository builds
  and publish the validated public artifact to the public Netlify site only.
- [ ] Enable the reviewed Website/CRM production gates in the agreed controlled
  order after the installed-system evidence passes. Confirm the permanent
  checkout accepts the intended household-order flow before promoting its link.
- [ ] Publish the Firewood order link, keep the phone alternative, and verify
  live pricing, HTTPS, mobile layout, privacy controls, SEO and estimate links
  with non-submitting smoke checks.
- [ ] Record exact deployed source/configuration identities and remaining
  operational limits. Preserve every provider and outbox idempotency identity.

If a release check fails, keep the affected collection/sending gates closed and
preserve accepted orders and provider evidence. After real orders exist, recover
the latest complete paired state or repair forward; do not restore a stale
pre-install database or replay uncertain messages with new identities.
